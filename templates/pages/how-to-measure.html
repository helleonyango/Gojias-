Before you start

  • Use a soft tape measure and keep it level around the body.
  • Measure over light clothing — snug, not tight (you should fit a finger under the tape where noted).
  • Stand straight and relaxed; ask someone to help for shoulder, back and length.
  • For length, wear the shoes/heels you plan to use.

Our order form accepts centimetres or inches (you can switch units there). This guide lists everything in inches.
